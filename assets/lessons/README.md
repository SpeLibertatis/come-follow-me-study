## Selection

The user requested the principle marked by the **seminary icon**, not the lesson's overall title or the children's section. The headings were checked in official 2026 manual chapters 46–52.

- Daniel: Daniel in the lions' den illustrates faith under trial.
- Hosea/Joel: Moroni's visit illustrates the manual's explicit connection between Joel's promise and Joseph Smith—History 1:41. Moroni is not presented as the Holy Ghost.
- Amos/Obadiah/Jonah: Moses with the tablets is identified as an example of revelation through prophets, not a portrait of Amos.
- Micah/Nahum/Habakkuk/Zephaniah: Jesus with His disciples in the storm illustrates Mark 4:37–38, expressly cross-referenced in the marked Habakkuk section. It is not a promise that every trial ends immediately.
- Haggai/Zechariah: the Provo City Center Temple is named and connects to the temple-priority discussion and video in the marked section; it is not portrayed as the ancient Jerusalem temple.
- Malachi: a family paying tithing illustrates faith through tithing without equating blessings with wealth.
- Christmas: the Nativity connects joy in the Redeemer with His birth.

## Processing and performance

Full compositions are preserved, with no cropping, recoloring, overlays, or retouching. The source renditions were proportionally resized to 320 and 640 pixels wide, encoded as WebP at quality 80, and stripped of metadata. Responsive `srcset`/`sizes` selects a local rendition. Intrinsic dimensions reserve space; all images except the first use native lazy loading, and all decode asynchronously. Desktop images display at 13rem; on phones they stack below the lesson text at up to 20rem. Alt text describes the scene; visible captions explain its relevance. No runtime dependencies, remote image requests, or CSP exceptions are introduced.

If reuse terms change or permission is withdrawn, remove the affected figure and its two files or replace it with a documented permitted asset. The surrounding lesson navigation works without images.
