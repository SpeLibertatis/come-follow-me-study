## Review guidance
Keep original supplied DOI links. Recheck blocked destinations in an ordinary browser before publication; do not replace restricted links with unauthorized mirrors. Do not turn abstract-level introductions into claims of comprehensive review. No research source replaces scripture or authoritative Church teaching. The site presents narrative claims, evidence, interpretations, teachings and uncertainty separately.
