# Joel narrative redesign — candidate

Audience: thoughtful students ages 13–15 studying independently. Current approved Daniel is the reference, not an editing target.

Problem: Joel previously asked learners to apply a promise before encountering its setting; #complete only announced that they could stop. This candidate uses evidence-before-explanation and four paced scenes: loss and return; presence and Spirit beyond status; the risen Christ at Pentecost and latter-day reach; quiet guidance. The close invites one concrete private practice and time, not a manufactured feeling.

Visual direction: original cinematic agricultural landscape; monumental Georgia and copper italic promise; ivory contextual reading with verse excerpts; teal close-reading phrase disclosures; editorial historical connection; deliberate personal close. Shared shell/tokens unchanged. No timers, scores, user input, tracking, persistence or autoplay. Native details work without JavaScript.

Sources: official 2026 manual chapter 47, Joel 1:4/10–12 and 2:12–13/21–32, Acts 2:16–18/32–33, Joseph Smith—History 1:41. Prior verified chains retained: manual → Scripture Helps → Hinckley; manual → Stevenson → Helaman 5:30 → 3 Nephi 9:20; manual → Scripture Helps → Nelson → Moroni 10:5. Scripture Helps supports the devastation setting and multiple fulfillments. KJV excerpts reproduce only the public-domain biblical wording, not modern headings/footnotes. Original summary and interpretation are distinguished from quoted text.

Guardrails: suffering is not proof of personal wrongdoing; restoration is not guaranteed on a student's timetable; all flesh does not mean identical gifts or that every thought is revelation. A repeated reading in optional deep study is explicitly for wider context/comparison.

Artwork: built-in imagegen original imagined Judean landscape, generated November 15, 2026. No real people, text, third-party source image or representation of the Holy Ghost. WebP renditions 1672×941 and 960×540, first-party; labeled imagined, not historical reconstruction.

Existing PDFs stay as concise offline companions; no files or routes removed. Candidate not published until reviewed and approved.
