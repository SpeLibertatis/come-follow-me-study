# Student privacy notice

This independent supplemental scripture study site does not request names, accounts, or contact details. Reflections are not sent to our servers or saved automatically. Refreshing or leaving the page normally clears text, although browsers can sometimes preserve page state temporarily; avoid entering identifying information on shared devices.

Students can explicitly copy thoughts to their system clipboard, open their device's native share sheet, or download a plain-text file. Clipboard contents may be accessible to other apps; downloads may remain on the device or in cloud backups; sharing destinations have their own privacy policies. Students control the destination. The site does not automatically transmit thoughts to any destination.

No analytics, advertisements, cookies, databases, or third-party scripts are included. The hosting provider and network intermediaries may process ordinary access logs including IP addresses. External scripture links follow the destination site's privacy practices.
