# Student privacy notice
This is an independent, unofficial supplemental scripture study resource. We do not request names, accounts, or contact details. We do not send or persist answers, reflections, or progress. Text typed into the reflection field is not automatically saved. Refreshing or leaving the page normally clears it; a browser may temporarily preserve page state, so avoid typing identifying information on shared devices. Browser back/forward cache restores are explicitly cleared where supported.

Students can choose to copy their thoughts to the system clipboard, share them using their device's native share sheet, or download a plain-text file. These actions require a user click. Clipboard contents may be available to other apps; files may remain in Downloads or cloud backups; shared content is governed by the destination app's privacy settings. The website does not choose a destination or transmit reflections to its own servers. No analytics, ads, cookies, or third-party scripts are included. The website host and network providers may process routine access logs, including IP addresses. External scripture links follow the destination site's privacy policy.
